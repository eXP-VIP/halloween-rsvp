One night. Two moods.

A hidden gem, revealed after dark

Discover a hidden gem tucked deep inside The Dean Munich, Autograph Collection by Marriott. True to its "Exactly like nothing else" spirit, this space has remained off-limits to the public and is now opening its doors for the first time.

Two distinct rooms, twin DJ setups and a dedicated bar set the scene—the perfect setting to bring your friends and share an unforgettable evening.

The Secret Party Room

High-energy DJ sets, heavy bass and an electric dance floor.

The Chill-Out Lounge

Its own bar, ambient music, signature drinks and quieter conversation.

Dinner before the party

Thinking of making your evening extra special? Start the night with a Japanese culinary experience at Ibasho, The Dean’s signature restaurant. Especially curated for our party guests, this 8-course dinner features fresh seafood and sushi, seasonal pumpkin specialities, robata-grilled beef and mochi.
