A hidden gem, revealed after dark

Discover a hidden gem tucked deep inside The Dean Munich, Autograph Collection by Marriott, a luxury design boutique hotel in Munich Westend. True to its "Exactly like nothing else" spirit, this space is normally off-limits to the public, opening exclusively for us for a rare, extraordinary night.

Two distinct rooms, twin DJ setups and two dedicated bars set the scene—the perfect setting to bring your friends and share an unforgettable evening.

The Secret Party Room

High-energy DJ sets, heavy bass and an electric dance floor.

The Chill-Out Lounge

Its own bar, ambient music, signature drinks and quieter conversation.
